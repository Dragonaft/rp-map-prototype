## Selecting Research is instant, completing it isn't

Picking a tech to research (in the Research modal) applies immediately — no turn wait. What isn't
instant is the tech itself: it accrues progress from your Research rate every income tick until
its cost is met, then completes automatically. Switching to a different tech mid-progress doesn't
lose your work — your progress on the old one is saved and picked back up if you return to it
later.

Your Research stat shown in the top bar is a **rate** (points per turn), not a stockpile — it's
recalculated every turn from your CAPITAL/LIBRARY buildings plus any Research-boosting tech
you've completed.

## Branches

- **Economy** — income bonuses, upkeep reduction, Goods-production throughput, building-cap
  increases on specific landscapes, supply range.
- **Military** — attack and defense bonus ladders, road/logistics range, supply range, Troop-pool
  rate, unlocking new Troop types.
- **Class branches** (NOBLE, HOLY, GUILD by default) — exclusive units, buildings, and abilities,
  each ending in that class's elite-unit capstone.

## Picking a class

You start classless. Researching a **class-root** tech locks you into that class permanently —
there's no switching afterward. Once classed, only that class's branch is visible to you; the
other two disappear from your Research tree. Economy and Military stay available to everyone
regardless of class.

## Prerequisites

Every tech lists what has to be researched first. Class-branch techs (beyond the root) also
require you to already hold that class. None of this is visible or selectable for a class an
admin has hidden from the current campaign.

## Effects are cumulative

A completed tech's bonuses apply for good — there's no maintenance cost, no risk of losing a
completed tech. Stack enough Research and the compounding bonuses (income, defense, supply range,
Troop-pool rate) become one of your strongest long-term levers.
