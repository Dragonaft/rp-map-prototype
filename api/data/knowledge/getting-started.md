## Claim your first Province

Click any unclaimed Province on the map to open the setup panel, then confirm. This is instant —
no turn wait. On confirmation you receive:

- A **CAPITAL** building, placed automatically on that Province
- **3000 Troops** and **5000 Money**
- **10 Research** and starting Goods: **200 Lumber**, **500 Food**

Your CAPITAL is your seat of power. It can't be demolished, produces Research and a trickle of
Food every turn, and counts as a supply source for your Armies (see **Supply & Food**).

## The four-verb loop

Everything else in the game builds on one cycle: **claim Provinces, build structures, deploy
Troops, and invade neighbors.**

1. **Territory** — Provinces are the base layer. Owning more of them means more buildings, more
   income, more Troops.
2. **Economy** — buildings turn your land into Money, Research, raw Resources, and manufactured
   Goods.
3. **Armies** — recruit Troops, move them, and fight for territory.
4. **Diplomacy** — wars, peace, alliances, and trade with other players, layered on top of the
   map/build/army loop.

## Where things live

There's one screen: the map. Every other surface — the build menu, army panels, the tech tree,
diplomacy, this Codex — opens as a modal on top of it. Nothing ever navigates you away from the
map.

- **Top bar** — your resource readouts (Research, Money, Troops, Piety if HOLY-class), Research,
  Diplomacy, and map-mode controls.
- **Province inspector** — click any Province to open its panel on the right. What you see there
  depends on your relationship to it: unclaimed, yours, occupied, or someone else's.
- **Notifications bell** — the only place you'll see *why* a queued action failed once the turn
  has processed.
