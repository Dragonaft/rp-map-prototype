## Running a negative treasury is dangerous, not free

Nothing stops your Money from going negative in the short term — upkeep and construction still
process even if they push you below zero. But the game tracks how many turns in a row you've
stayed negative, and there's a hard consequence for letting it run too long.

## The countdown

Every turn your treasury ends negative, you'll get a **Bankruptcy Risk** warning in your
Notifications with a turns-remaining count. The moment your Money is zero or positive again, the
counter resets to zero — there's no lingering penalty for a single bad turn.

Stay negative for **more than 4 consecutive turns** and bankruptcy triggers on the turn after.

## What happens on bankruptcy

- **Every one of your Armies is disbanded outright.**
- Your Money is reset to **0**.
- Any recurring trade treaty where *you* send money is cancelled.
- For the next **6 turns**, you're debuffed: your combat power is cut by **50%** in any battle,
  and your buildings stop producing both Goods and raw resources entirely.

You'll get a **Bankruptcy Declared** notification explaining exactly what happened. The debuff
counts down on its own — there's nothing to do but wait it out and rebuild.

## Staying solvent

Bankruptcy is a hard floor, not something you drift into by accident if you're watching your
resource bar. Before it hits, you have at least 4 full turns of warning notifications — demolish
an unaffordable building, disband an Army you can't support, or draw down a treaty, and the
counter resets the instant your treasury turns positive again.
