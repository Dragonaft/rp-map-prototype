## Four core resources, and Research

Every turn your CAPITAL and LIBRARY buildings generate the resources you run your country on:

| Resource | Generated by | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **Money** | Building income (mines, farms, etc.) | Construction, recruitment, upkeep |
| **Troops** | BARRACKS/CAPITAL draft pool | Creating and reinforcing Armies |
| **Piety** | Temples (10/turn), Cathedrals (20/turn) | HOLY-class recruitment (Paladins, Templar Order) |
| **Research** | CAPITAL + LIBRARY (10 each, Research-scaled) | Unlocking Tech |

Piety only shows in your resource bar if you're HOLY-class. Research is a **per-turn rate**, not
a stockpile — see **Research & Classes** for how it accrues.

## Raw resources vs. manufactured Goods

Don't confuse these two systems:

- **Raw resources** (iron, gold, stone, wood, grain, fish) are mined or harvested straight from a
  Province's deposit into your national stockpile by buildings like MINE, FORESTRY, and BARN.
- **Goods** (Lumber, Food, Weapons, Bricks, and the three class prestige goods) are
  **manufactured** — a building consumes raw resources and turns them into a Good each turn.

Raw resources are the input; Goods are the output. See **Goods & Production** for the full chain.

## Income

Every building has a flat Money income value at its seed data — MINE included. Raw resource
stockpiles feed manufacturing, not your treasury directly. Research can boost income generically
(e.g. an economy tech multiplying income by a flat percentage), and separately reduce upkeep the
same way.

## Upkeep

Every building charges its own upkeep every turn — there's no free tier. Armies cost a flat base
upkeep plus a per-unit cost scaled to their Troop composition. Falling behind on upkeep for too
long risks bankruptcy — see **Money & Bankruptcy**.
